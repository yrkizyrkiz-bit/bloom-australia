# Product brief — Sanative Health

## One-liner
Doctor-led preventative health membership: biomarker testing, telehealth consults, and personalised care pathways — starting at **$1/day ($365 billed annually)**.

## Positioning signals (current marketing)
- Homepage hero: **“Doctor-led care with biomarkers insight”**
- Membership: **“One membership. Full clarity.”**
- Labs: monitor early indicators / know your numbers
- Lifestyle banner: **“Advanced biomarker reporting and AI insights to help you live healthier, longer.”**
- Journey: Check health → Doctor consult → Results → Action plan

## Core offer
| Element | Current claim |
|---------|----------------|
| Price | $1/day, $365 billed annually |
| Panel | Essential / Advanced messaging varies by surface (see consistency risks) |
| Biomarker count | Marketing uses **70+** (membership card) and **85+** (homepage/labs elsewhere) |
| Clinical | AHPRA-registered Australian doctors |
| Included perk | First **30 days** of an eligible care program with membership |
| Tracking | Organ Care dashboards / Biological Clock in member portal |

## Care programs (post-membership pathways)
1. Weight Management  
2. Hair Health  
3. Men’s Health (incl. sexual health / ED subpaths)  
4. Women’s Health (incl. menopause)  
5. Metabolic / Organ Care (fatty liver, heart, kidney, PCOS)

## Ideal customer profile (inferred from site)
- Australian adults seeking proactive health insights  
- People stuck on weight, energy, hormones, hair, or metabolic concerns  
- Prefer online doctor access over waiting rooms  
- Willing to pay annually for testing + ongoing digital care  

## Business model (inferred)
- Annual membership (Stripe) → pathology + doctor review → optional program pathways  
- Clinic/referral join (`/join`) and GP/for-doctors surfaces exist  

## Brand / tech posture
- Visual language: sage, cream, terracotta; Cormorant/Playfair display type  
- Stack: Next.js 15.5, Prisma, NextAuth, Stripe, Netlify  
- SEO: intentionally **noindex** (pre-public launch)
